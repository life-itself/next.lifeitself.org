Two winters ago we set out to plant an orchard on the big field above the [Life Itself Farmhouse](/hubs/farmhouse), near Thénac in the Dordogne. We got a soil analysis, a layout for 68 trees, a variety list, a load of donkey manure and a lot of good advice. Then spring arrived, and not a single tree went in the ground.

This December we are trying again, smaller and with fixed dates.

## What we are planting

About twenty organic, bare-root trees from a small nursery in Lalinde, half an hour up the Dordogne:

* **plums**, which do very well here: Prune d'Agen, greengages, damsons and a Dordogne variety;
* **local apples**, such as Court pendu gris de Dordogne and Reinette de Brive;
* **quince, medlar, figs, a persimmon and a mulberry**;
* **two young walnuts**, from local seed;
* and a few nitrogen-fixing shrubs to shelter them.

We plan to collect them at the Lalinde tree market at the end of November and plant in the first two weeks of December.

## What we learnt the first time

Looking back at [what happened in 2024/25](https://farmhouse.lifeitself.org/trees/history), three things stalled it:

* **The dates slipped.** We waited on the plan, then the manure, then the variety list, and the planting window closed.
* **Good ideas arrived late.** Berms and swales to manage water are a fine idea, but they raised the question of whether to do earthworks first, and that question stopped everything.
* **Nobody owned it on site.** Plenty of people helped, but no one person was driving it.

So this time:

* **Small first.** Twenty trees, not sixty-eight. If deer or a dry summer take some, we learn something cheaply. More next winter, once we know what thrives.
* **Plant first, perfect later.** No earthworks before planting: a hand-dug lip below each tree to catch water, thick mulch, and a proper swale later if we need one.
* **Local and tough.** French and Dordogne varieties on vigorous rootstocks, bought young, so they root deep and need little care.
* **Fixed dates.** Order this week, collect at the end of the month, plant within two weeks.

That is the plan; details may change as we go.

## Follow along, or come and help

We have started a small [Farmhouse knowledge base](https://farmhouse.lifeitself.org) with notes on [the land](https://farmhouse.lifeitself.org/land), [the soil](https://farmhouse.lifeitself.org/soil) and [the tree planting](https://farmhouse.lifeitself.org/trees), including [the full plan](https://farmhouse.lifeitself.org/trees/plan) and [dated updates](https://farmhouse.lifeitself.org/trees/updates).

If you are nearby in early December and would like to help plant, or could help look after young trees through the spring, we would love to hear from you. Please [get in touch](/contact); we will post the planting dates on the [updates page](https://farmhouse.lifeitself.org/trees/updates).
