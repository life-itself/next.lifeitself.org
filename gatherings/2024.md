The 2024 Gathering marked the return of Life Itself's original annual event format, with a theme centered on the Second Renaissance and the question of how to nurture the seeds of a new world.

It combined a first part focused on sharing vision through talks, workshops, and open space, and a second part focused on co-creation, projects, and alliances.

- [Read the original 2024 announcement](/blog/announcing-gathering-2024)

## Highlights

- Hosted at the Life Itself Farmhouse Hub near Bergerac
- Focus on art, politics, spirituality, and cultural transformation
- Two-part structure: shared exploration followed by collaborative creation
