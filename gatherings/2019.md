The 2019 Gathering brought around thirty people together in La Cheraille for a week of shared inquiry, discussion, and community life.

The programme explored major themes such as rationality, individuality, equality, education, climate, co-living, and the practical challenge of building wiser ways of living together.

- [Read the full 2019 recap](/blog/our-2019-gathering)

## Highlights

- Talks, workshops, and embodied activities across a full week
- A strong emphasis on community, reflection, and interdisciplinary exchange
- Ongoing exploration of wisdom as both a personal and social practice
