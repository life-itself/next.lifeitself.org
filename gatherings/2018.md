The 2018 Gathering marked the third year of this recurring Art / Earth / Tech and Life Itself format. People came from around the world to La Cheraille near Rambouillet, France, for a week of talks, discussions, embodied practices, and shared life.

Themes included education, conscious co-living, wellbeing, technology, aesthetics, and wider questions about how to live wisely and well together.

- [Read the full 2018 recap](/blog/our-2018-gathering)

## Highlights

- Talks and discussions spanning education, culture, technology, and wellbeing
- Embodied practices including meditation, yoga, ecstatic dance, and sharing circles
- Early explorations of themes that continued to shape Life Itself in later years
