Life Itself publishes research papers, white papers, and reports exploring paths to a wiser, weller world. Our work spans collective intelligence, wellbeing, contemplative traditions, and systemic change.

## White Papers

- **Collective Intelligence** - Clarifies the concept of collective intelligence and argues for the pre-eminence of emotional factors in group intelligence. [Download](https://lifeitself.org/download/4441/)
- **Collective Wellbeing** - Drawing on ancient wisdom, current science, and history, this paper explores why wellbeing is denied its proper place in public life and suggests remedies. [Download](https://lifeitself.org/download/4445/)
- **The Fate of "The Master and His Emissary"** - An analysis of Iain McGilchrist's work and its reception, exploring the neural underpinnings of mindful presence. [Download](https://lifeitself.org/download/4458/)
