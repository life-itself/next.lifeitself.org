A list of presentations given by members of the Life Itself Research community.

- [Jonah Wilberg on Wise Altruism](https://news.lifeitself.org/p/jonah-wilberg-on-wise-altruism) (Research Community Call, May 2025). Jonah Wilberg shares his research on how the cognitive science of wisdom can serve an expanded notion of Effective Altruism. 
- [Momcilo Mijovic on Sensemaking, Blindspots and Systems Contradictions](https://news.lifeitself.org/p/research-community-call-momcilo-mijovic) (Research Community Call, May 2025). Momcilo Mijovic shares a brief summary of his research into sense-making, blindspots and the process of surfacing societal contradictions.
- [Simon Grant on Ontological Commoning](https://news.lifeitself.org/p/simon-grant-on-ontological-commoning) (Research Community Call, April 2025). Simon Grant explores the process and practice of finding common ground between different perspectives on a complex situation.
- [Matthew McCarthy on Navigating the Epistemological Delirium](https://news.lifeitself.org/p/matthew-mccarthy-on-navigating-the) (Research Community Call, March 2025). Matthew McCarthy explores how the dominant values behind knowledge production might be re-imagined in the coming years.
- [Momcilo Mijovic on Pierre Bourdieu and Common Sense](https://news.lifeitself.org/p/momcilo-mijovic-on-pierre-bourdieu) (Research Community Call, February 2025). Momcilo Mijovic explores Pierre Bourdieu and his analysis of society as a continuously spinning cycle of re-production.
- [Jonah Wilberg on Paradigm Shifts and Progress in Cultural Evolution](https://news.lifeitself.org/p/jonah-wilberg-on-paradigm-shifts) (Research Community Call, February 2025). Jonah Wilberg explores the idea of progress in cultural evolution, in relation to Thomas Kuhn’s notion of scientific paradigm shifts.
- [Jamie Bristow on Exploring Societal Resilience in the Face of the Polycrisis](https://news.lifeitself.org/p/jamie-bristow-on-exploring-societal) (Research Community Call, December 2024). Jamie Bristow explores the critical role of psychosocial resilience in the face of crisis and collapse.
- [Nicholas Wilkinson on our Relationship to Nature in a Mass Extinction](https://news.lifeitself.org/p/nicholas-wilkinson-on-mass-extinction) (Research Community Call, November 2024). Nicholas Wilkinson dives into the the complex relationship between humans and nature.
- [Rufus Pollock on Christopher Alexander, a Second Renaissance Visionary](https://news.lifeitself.org/p/christopher-alexander-second-renaissance) (Research Community Call, July 2024). Brown bag talk about Christopher Alexander as a pioneering Second Renaissance (aka Integral, Metamodern etc) thinker and practitioner.
- [Tom Morgan on Finding Purpose Beyond Wealth and Expectations](https://news.lifeitself.org/p/life-itself-research-community-forum) (Research Community Call, April 2024). Tom Morgan delves into the fusion of spirituality and materialism for individual and societal growth.
- [Boaz Feldman on Nervous System Approaches for Resilient Community Living](https://news.lifeitself.org/p/boaz-feldman-and-nervous-system-approaches) (ICSA Virtual Conference, January 2024).
