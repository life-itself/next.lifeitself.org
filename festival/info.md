        <h3 class="font-restora text-3xl font-semibold">Driving</h3>
        <p class="mt-4 text-lg leading-relaxed">The Farmhouse is a twenty-minute drive from Bergerac and around one hour forty minutes from Bordeaux.</p>
        <p class="mt-3 text-lg leading-relaxed">It is a five-minute drive or twenty-five-minute walk from Plum Village Monastery.</p>
      </section>
      <section>
        <h3 class="font-restora text-3xl font-semibold">Cycling</h3>
        <p class="mt-4 text-lg leading-relaxed">The Farmhouse is around one hour twenty minutes by bike from Bergerac and Sainte-Foy-la-Grande.</p>
        <p class="mt-3"><a href="https://www.freewheelingfrance.com/where-to-go/?region=Aquitaine" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer" class="underline underline-offset-4">Cycling routes in Aquitaine</a></p>

        <ul class="mt-4 list-disc space-y-2 pl-5 text-lg leading-relaxed">
          <li>Aéroport Bergerac Dordogne Périgord — 25 minutes by car</li>
          <li>Bordeaux Airport — about 1 hour 25 minutes by car</li>
        </ul>
        <h4 class="mt-8 font-restora text-2xl font-semibold">Bordeaux Airport</h4>
        <p class="mt-4 text-lg leading-relaxed">From Bordeaux Airport, guests can take a bus to Bordeaux Saint-Jean station and then a TER train to Bergerac. The train takes about one hour fifteen minutes. From Bergerac, the Hub is a twenty-minute taxi ride.</p>
