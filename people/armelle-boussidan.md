Armelle is a facilitator, consultant and artist with a PhD in Cognitive Science and Linguistics from University of Lyon/CNRS. She brings cross-cultural sensitivity to multilingual projects spanning ecological, cultural and well-being sectors, integrating scientific rigor with creative sensibility.

Her background combines training in permaculture and contemplative practices with years of community-driven projects. She is also a self-taught painter, exhibiting since 2011.
