# Developmental Spaces

A deliberately developmental space is a community, programme or place whose core purpose is to support the inner growth and development of the people in it: cognitive, emotional, relational and spiritual. Life Itself's hubs and residencies are experiments in this kind of space.

Learn more at [developmentalspaces.org](https://developmentalspaces.org/), and see our work on [[initiatives/building-the-field-for-developmental-spaces|building the field for developmental spaces]].
