The Gathering is one of Life Itself's enduring formats: a recurring space for coming together around wise living, inner development, community, culture, and social transformation.

Some editions are more outward-facing and event-oriented; others are more reflective, experimental, or community-rooted. Across the years, the Gathering has remained a place to meet, practice, think, create, and imagine wiser worlds together.
