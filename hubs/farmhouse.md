      <h2 class="font-restora text-3xl font-semibold">A Different Way of Building</h2>
      <p class="font-restora text-lg italic text-secondary">This wasn't about designing buildings. It was about designing life.</p>
      <p>Most projects start with architects and digital renderings. At the Farmhouse, we chose a different path. Inspired by <a href="https://news.lifeitself.org/p/christopher-alexander-second-renaissance" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer">Christopher Alexander</a>, we listened to the land and shaped a vision through clay models, hand sketches, and conversation.</p>
      <p>We asked: Is this spot alive? Does this pathway breathe? Does this structure belong to the land? Each answer shaped what followed.</p>

      <p class="font-restora text-lg italic text-secondary">Set in Thenac in southwest rural France.</p>
      <p>Our 8,000 m² site blends heritage buildings with new spaces for growth and connection. Ancient trees, surrounding fields, and the nearby <a href="https://plumvillage.org/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer">Plum Village Monastery</a> create an atmosphere that is contemplative and alive.</p>
      <p>We let the land lead us: the fire circle where sunsets gather, the slope that carries energy, and the still corners that invite quiet.</p>
