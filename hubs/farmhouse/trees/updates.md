News on [fruit tree planting at the Farmhouse](/hubs/farmhouse/trees), newest first.

## 25 November 2026: order drafted

We have drafted an order for 20 trees and 4 shrubs from Le Goût des Arbres, a small organic nursery in Lalinde: plums, local apples, a pear, quince, medlar, figs, walnuts, persimmon and mulberry. We plan to collect them at the Lalinde tree market on Saturday 28 November (to be confirmed). See [the plan](/hubs/farmhouse/trees/plan).

## 22 November 2026: planting is back on

Two years after our [first try](/hubs/farmhouse/trees/history) stalled, we are restarting. The aim: a small first planting of about twenty trees in the ground by mid-December, protected from deer, with care arranged through the winter and spring.

What is different this time: a smaller first planting, a local nursery with fixed dates, and no earthworks before we plant.
